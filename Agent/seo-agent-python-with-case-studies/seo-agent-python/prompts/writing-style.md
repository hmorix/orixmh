# Writing Style Rules

> This document defines how every piece of HMorix content should be written. These rules apply to blogs, landing pages, documentation, product pages, tutorials, emails, newsletters, press releases, and marketing copy.

---

# Writing Philosophy

Write for people first.

Search engines should reward helpful content—not be the primary audience.

Every article should solve a problem, answer a question, or help readers make a better decision.

---

# Tone

Write like an experienced engineer, consultant, or technical practitioner talking to another professional.

The tone should be:

- Direct
- Confident
- Helpful
- Honest
- Practical
- Professional
- Friendly
- Engineering-led

Never sound:

- Salesy
- Overly corporate
- Academic
- Robotic
- Overly enthusiastic

---

# Sentence Length

Mix sentence lengths naturally.

Recommended:

- Short: 5–10 words
- Medium: 15–25 words
- Long: Only when necessary

Avoid writing three or more long sentences consecutively.

Rhythm matters.

---

# Paragraph Rules

Body paragraphs should contain:

- 2–4 sentences
- One primary idea
- Logical flow

Avoid large blocks of text.

White space improves readability.

---

# Opening Rules

Never begin with:

- A question
- The article title rewritten
- Generic background
- AI clichés
- Marketing fluff

Instead:

Start with:

- A useful fact
- A practical observation
- A common problem
- A real-world scenario
- A concise statement

Example:

"Slow websites lose visitors long before the design has a chance to impress."

---

# Headings

Use sentence case.

Good:

How AI helps automate repetitive business tasks

Avoid:

How AI Helps Automate Repetitive Business Tasks

Headings should describe exactly what the section covers.

Avoid vague headings like:

- Benefits
- Features
- Improvements
- Overview

Prefer:

- How AI reduces manual work
- Choosing the right automation workflow
- When custom software makes sense

---

# Writing Style

Prefer:

- Active voice
- Concrete examples
- Specific explanations
- Practical recommendations
- Honest trade-offs
- Real business scenarios

Avoid:

- Empty filler
- Marketing buzzwords
- Generic advice
- Repeating the same idea
- Keyword stuffing

---

# Tables

Use tables when comparing:

- Three or more products
- Pricing plans
- Technologies
- Features
- Service packages
- Pros and cons

Do not use tables for simple explanations.

---

# Lists

Use bullet lists only when presenting:

- Steps
- Features
- Checklists
- Requirements
- Options

Do not replace explanatory paragraphs with unnecessary lists.

---

# Examples

Whenever possible:

Explain concepts using examples.

Instead of:

"Automation improves efficiency."

Write:

"An invoice approval workflow can automatically notify managers, reducing manual follow-up."

---

# Technical Content

Explain technical concepts simply.

Assume readers may not be developers.

Always define unfamiliar terminology.

Avoid unnecessary acronyms.

---

# Product References

Only reference HMorix products or services that exist in the knowledge base.

Never invent:

- Features
- Integrations
- Roadmaps
- Performance claims
- Pricing

---

# SEO Integration

Keywords should appear naturally.

Never write sentences solely to include keywords.

Content quality is always more important than keyword density.

---

# Banned Words & Phrases

Never use:

- unlock
- delve
- in today's landscape
- in today's fast-paced world
- game-changer
- revolutionize
- seamlessly
- at the end of the day
- it's worth noting
- in conclusion
- let's dive in
- cutting-edge
- world-class
- next-generation
- best-in-class
- one-stop solution
- ultimate solution
- magic
- guaranteed success
- unbeatable
- industry-leading (unless verified)

Replace hype with evidence.

---

# Calls to Action

Every content piece should end with:

## Option 1

Key Takeaways

or

## Option 2

Frequently Asked Questions

Choose whichever better suits the content.

Then finish with:

A single, relevant call to action that references a verified HMorix service or product from the knowledge base.

Example:

"Need a custom AI-powered website or automation solution? Contact HMorix to discuss your project."

Never promote products that are not listed in `products.md`.

---

# Human Writing Rules

The writing should feel like it was written by an experienced professional.

Vary:

- Sentence length
- Vocabulary
- Paragraph size
- Structure

Avoid repetitive transitions.

---

# Editing Checklist

Before finalizing content, verify:

- Clear introduction
- Logical flow
- Accurate information
- One H1 only
- Specific headings
- Natural keyword usage
- No keyword stuffing
- No banned phrases
- Short paragraphs
- Helpful examples
- Proper internal links (when applicable)
- Authoritative external references (when needed)
- Key Takeaways or FAQ section
- Relevant HMorix call to action

---

# Final Rule

Every article should leave readers with useful knowledge, realistic expectations, and confidence that HMorix values accuracy, clarity, and long-term trust over marketing hype.
