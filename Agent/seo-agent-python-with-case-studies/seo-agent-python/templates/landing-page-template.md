# Template: Landing Page Copy

> This template defines the standard structure for HMorix landing pages. Every landing page should focus on a single service, product, or business goal. The objective is to educate visitors, build trust, answer objections, and encourage meaningful conversions without relying on exaggerated marketing claims.

---

# Purpose

A HMorix landing page should:

- Clearly communicate the value of a service or product.
- Explain how it works.
- Help visitors determine whether it's right for them.
- Build credibility using only verified information.
- Encourage users to take the next step.

The page should educate first and sell second.

---

# Landing Page Structure

## 1. SEO Metadata

Generate before writing.

Include:

- SEO Title (under 60 characters)
- Meta Description (under 160 characters)
- Primary Keyword
- Secondary Keywords
- URL Slug
- Suggested Category

---

## 2. H1

Requirements:

- Focus on the customer's desired outcome.
- Mention the primary keyword naturally.
- Be clear and specific.
- Avoid feature-focused headlines.

Good Examples:

- Build a website that helps your business grow
- Automate repetitive business tasks with AI
- Custom software built around your workflow

Avoid:

- AI Solutions
- Our Features
- Welcome to HMorix

---

## 3. Subheadline

One concise sentence explaining:

- Who the service is for.
- What value it delivers.
- Why it matters.

Example:

Designed for businesses that need reliable software, AI automation, and long-term technical support without unnecessary complexity.

---

## 4. Primary Call to Action

Place above the fold.

Examples:

- Request a Consultation
- Talk to Our Team
- Get a Custom Quote
- Start Your Project

The CTA should describe the action clearly.

---

## 5. Benefits Section

Create 3–4 H2 sections.

Each section must include:

- The customer benefit.
- How HMorix delivers it.
- Why it matters.
- A practical example where appropriate.

Do not list benefits without explaining the mechanism.

Example:

### Reduce repetitive work with automation

Explain how automation replaces manual workflows and improves consistency instead of simply claiming "save time."

---

## 6. How It Works

Explain the process clearly.

Typical structure:

1. Understand your requirements.
2. Design the solution.
3. Develop and test.
4. Launch.
5. Provide ongoing support.

Only describe processes that are actually offered by HMorix.

---

## 7. Features

If appropriate, include a comparison table.

Example:

| Capability | Included |
|------------|----------|
| Custom Design | ✓ |
| Responsive Layout | ✓ |
| AI Integration | When applicable |
| Ongoing Support | As agreed |

Never invent features.

---

## 8. Who It's For

Describe ideal customers.

Examples:

- Small Businesses
- Startups
- Agencies
- Content Creators
- Enterprises

Explain why the service fits each audience.

---

## 9. Why Choose HMorix

Highlight verified differentiators such as:

- AI-first development
- Custom software
- Engineering-led approach
- Transparent communication
- Long-term support
- Practical solutions

Never claim to be the "best" or use unverifiable superlatives.

---

## 10. Social Proof

Only include:

- Verified testimonials
- Verified customer logos
- Verified case studies
- Verified awards
- Verified certifications

If none exist, use:

**Placeholder:** *Customer testimonials and project success stories will appear here once verified.*

Never invent testimonials, client names, ratings, or logos.

---

## 11. FAQ

Include 4–6 questions addressing common objections.

Examples:

- How long does a project usually take?
- Can existing software be integrated?
- Is ongoing support available?
- Can the solution scale as my business grows?
- Do you work with international clients?

Answer using only verified information.

---

## 12. Final Call to Action

End with a concise section.

Include:

- A short reminder of the value offered.
- One clear CTA.

Example:

Ready to discuss your project? Contact HMorix to explore a solution tailored to your business needs.

---

## 13. Suggested Images

Recommend visuals throughout the page.

For each image include:

- Placement
- Filename
- Alt text
- Optional caption

Prefer:

- Product screenshots
- Dashboards
- Workflow diagrams
- Team collaboration
- Business illustrations

Avoid generic stock imagery whenever possible.

---

## 14. Structured Data

Generate valid Schema.org JSON-LD.

Preferred types:

- WebPage
- Service
- Product (when appropriate)
- FAQPage (if FAQ is included)

Include all relevant structured data fields supported by the content.

---

# Conversion Guidelines

Each section should answer one customer question:

- What is it?
- Who is it for?
- How does it work?
- Why should I trust it?
- What happens next?

Reduce uncertainty instead of increasing urgency.

---

# Writing Rules

- Write naturally.
- Explain benefits with evidence or mechanisms.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use active voice.
- Avoid marketing clichés.
- Never invent facts.
- Follow the HMorix brand voice.
- Focus on clarity over persuasion.

---

# Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- One H1 only
- Outcome-focused headline
- Clear subheadline
- Strong primary CTA
- 3–4 benefit sections with concrete explanations
- Process or "How it works" section
- Feature table (when helpful)
- Audience section
- Verified differentiators only
- No invented testimonials or logos
- FAQ included
- Final CTA included
- SEO metadata completed
- Schema.org JSON-LD generated
- No unsupported claims or exaggerated language

---

# Final Principle

Every HMorix landing page should clearly explain the problem, demonstrate how the solution works, build trust through verified information, answer common objections, and guide visitors toward the next step with confidence and transparency.
