# Template: Standard Blog Post

> This template defines the standard structure for HMorix blog articles. Every blog should follow this format unless another template is explicitly requested.

---

# Purpose

The goal of every HMorix blog is to:

- Answer the reader's search intent.
- Teach something useful.
- Build trust through accurate information.
- Improve search visibility.
- Encourage readers to explore relevant HMorix services or products.

The article should prioritize clarity and usefulness over word count.

---

# Blog Structure

## 1. SEO Metadata

Generate before writing the article.

Include:

- SEO Title (under 60 characters)
- Primary Keyword
- Secondary Keywords
- Meta Description (under 160 characters)
- URL Slug
- Canonical URL (if applicable)
- Suggested Category
- Suggested Tags

---

## 2. H1

Requirements:

- One H1 only
- Primary keyword near the beginning
- Clear and descriptive
- Human-friendly

Example:

# AI website development for small businesses

---

## 3. Opening Paragraph

Length:

2–3 short paragraphs.

Requirements:

- Introduce the topic immediately.
- Explain why it matters.
- Include the primary keyword naturally.
- Match search intent.
- Avoid generic introductions.
- Do not ask questions.
- Do not repeat the title.

---

## 4. Table of Contents

Include only when the article exceeds **1,500 words**.

Use anchor links where supported.

---

## 5. Main Content

Use H2 headings for major topics.

Use H3 headings only when additional structure improves clarity.

Every section should:

- Teach something useful.
- Include practical advice.
- Explain real-world examples.
- Build logically from the previous section.

Avoid filler.

---

## 6. Comparisons

When discussing multiple options, include a comparison table.

Examples:

- Products
- Technologies
- Services
- Frameworks
- Pricing
- Advantages
- Limitations

Only use tables when they genuinely improve understanding.

---

## 7. Examples

Whenever appropriate include:

- Code snippets
- Business examples
- Practical workflows
- Step-by-step explanations
- Best practices
- Common mistakes

Examples should be realistic and easy to understand.

---

## 8. Images

Suggest relevant images throughout the article.

For each image provide:

- Placement
- Suggested filename
- Alt text
- Caption (optional)

Prefer diagrams, screenshots, workflows, and illustrations over decorative images.

---

## 9. Internal Links

Suggest relevant HMorix pages.

Examples:

- /
- /about
- /services
- /products
- /blogs
- /contact

Only recommend pages that are relevant to the topic.

---

## 10. External References

When factual claims require support, recommend authoritative sources.

Examples:

- Google Search Central
- MDN
- W3C
- OWASP
- OpenAI
- Microsoft Learn
- Node.js
- React
- PostgreSQL

Do not cite unreliable websites.

---

## 11. FAQ

Include:

3–5 questions.

Questions should reflect:

- Google People Also Ask
- Common customer concerns
- Practical implementation questions

Answers should:

- Be concise
- Be factual
- Avoid repetition

---

## 12. Key Takeaways

End every article with a short summary.

Options:

Bullet list

or

Two short paragraphs.

Summarize the most important ideas without introducing new information.

---

## 13. Call to Action

Finish with a single sentence.

The CTA must reference a verified HMorix service or product from the knowledge base.

Examples:

- AI Solutions
- Web Design
- Software Development
- BillingFlow
- AI Agent

Do not promote products that are not listed in `products.md`.

Keep the CTA helpful rather than sales-focused.

---

## 14. Structured Data

Generate valid Schema.org JSON-LD.

Preferred types:

- BlogPosting
- Article

Include:

- headline
- description
- author
- publisher
- datePublished
- dateModified
- image
- keywords
- mainEntityOfPage

---

# Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- One H1 only
- Search intent satisfied
- Primary keyword naturally placed
- Logical heading hierarchy
- Helpful examples
- No keyword stuffing
- No AI clichés
- No invented facts
- Relevant comparison table (when useful)
- Internal links suggested
- External references suggested
- Image alt text included
- FAQ section included
- Key Takeaways included
- CTA references a verified HMorix service or product
- SEO metadata completed
- Schema.org JSON-LD generated

---

# Final Principle

Every HMorix blog should teach something practical, remain technically accurate, follow modern SEO best practices, and leave readers with clear, actionable knowledge while maintaining the HMorix brand voice.
