Core Enterprise Products & Platforms

1. BillingFlow – Invoicing & Financial Operations

Next-generation automated invoicing, multi-currency billing, real-time Indian GST calculation (CGST, SGST, IGST), automated payment reminders via WhatsApp and email, and proactive subscription recovery for SMEs and high-growth enterprises.

2. AI Agent Platform – Autonomous Enterprise Workflows

Autonomous conversational agents and business process orchestration powered by NVIDIA NIM (Llama 3.1 405B) and domain-specific fine-tuned models. Features RAG document intelligence, customer support ticket triage, and seamless CRM synchronization.

3. PDF Automation – High-Throughput Document Processing

Programmatic document generation engine producing legally compliant tax invoices, employee salary slips, offer letters, joining contracts, and digital certificates at scale with cryptographic signature verification.

4. Enterprise HRM & Global Payroll Suite

Unified Human Resource Management featuring biometric/geo-fenced attendance tracking, ATS recruitment pipeline, multi-tiered leave approvals, and Indian statutory payroll calculation (PF, ESI, TDS, PT).
